Modular Norm RandOpt: Population-Efficient Ensembling through Architecture-Aware Perturbations

Kirato Yoshihara, Hiroaki Hamade

Under review at NeurReps, a NeurIPS 2026 Workshop
Extended Abstract Track

We replace isotropic weight perturbations with architecture-aware sampling to improve the accuracy–population tradeoff of language model ensembles.

About this work +

Modular Norm RandOpt normalizes each parameter perturbation with a role-specific norm and rescales it using a fixed profile derived from model architecture and source-task sensitivity calibration. It preserves RandOpt’s candidate ranking and ensembling procedure.

On Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, the method outperforms RandOpt using 3× fewer candidate models on Countdown and at least 12× fewer on GSM8K. Across seven tasks and three Qwen model sizes, it improves on RandOpt in 14 of 21 settings and ties in one additional setting.
